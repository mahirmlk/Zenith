5. Complete the implementation-plan approval and isolated execution workflow before enabling paper-to-code execution; keep arbitrary execution disabled until that review is complete.
6. Complete invitation delivery and collaboration UX if they are required for the release scope.
7. Complete the remaining product scope in `update.md`: legally-held PDF ingestion and page/section evidence, broader synthesis and claim verification, collaboration/invitation delivery, expanded watch targets, and a full reliability/quality evaluation baseline. Current evidence is limited to abstract-level spans and is not a scientific synthesis.
